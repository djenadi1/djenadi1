### Projects

| Project | What it does | Result |
|---|---|---|
| [Alpine Snow Depth Estimation](https://github.com/djenadi1/FULL-PROJECTS/tree/main/ASDE) | Corrects Depth Anything 3 in snow with LiDAR, trained on my own Unreal Engine 5 renders, and flies a simulated drone across a valley with no map | AbsRel 0.977 → 0.387 on renders |
| [Vehicle Speed Estimation](https://github.com/djenadi1/FULL-PROJECTS/tree/main/SPEEDESTIM) | Measures each car's speed from a fixed traffic camera, with a dataset of 150 rendered freeway cameras | Mask mAP50 0.884 on unseen road |
| [TikTok AI Content Detector](https://github.com/djenadi1/FULL-PROJECTS/tree/main/Tiktok) | Flags AI-generated TikTok posts from metadata and captions, from a 99-million-post dataset | ROC-AUC 0.841 at the real 5.8% AI rate |
| [PCB Defect Detector](https://github.com/djenadi1/FULL-PROJECTS/tree/main/PCBD) | Finds 10 defect types on full circuit-board photos with tiled YOLO, as a [live app](https://full-projects-fwgz2ytdquksf6mexeldqg.streamlit.app/) | mAP50 0.90 |

All of them are in [FULL-PROJECTS](https://github.com/djenadi1/FULL-PROJECTS).
